Memorandum of Understanding: Dynasty Arena
1. Purpose & Scope
The parties (Boots/Hogan and Wombi/Stephen) agree to collaborate on the development of Dynasty Arena. The scope of the project is defined exclusively by the Game Design Document (GDD), which is attached to this agreement as "Appendix A." Any features or content not described in the GDD are considered outside the current scope. Any additions to the scope must be agreed upon in writing by both parties via an amendment to the GDD.

2. IP & Ownership
Boots (Hogan) shall retain sole and exclusive ownership of all underlying engine technology, codebases, assets, models, and textures created for the project. Wombi (Stephen) is granted a license to these assets strictly for the purposes of the commercial exploitation of Dynasty Arena as defined in this agreement.

3. Financial Terms
Investment: Wombi (Stephen) agrees to provide an advance of $10,000 NZD to support the development of the game.

Profit Sharing: The parties (Boots/Hogan and Wombi/Stephen) agree to a 50/50 split of the Net Profits.

Definition: Net Profit is defined as Gross Revenue received from all storefronts (e.g., Steam) minus platform fees (e.g., 30% Steam cut), transaction costs, and applicable taxes.

Payment Cycle: Profit distributions will be managed by Boots (Hogan) on a monthly basis, triggered by the date Steam releases funds. Boots (Hogan) will provide Wombi (Stephen) with documentation (e.g., store dashboard screenshots) confirming the revenue for each period prior to payment. Wombi (Stephen) may have access to the steam dashboard to confirm the revenue for each period prior to payment if he desires.

4. Operational Roles
Boots (Hogan): Responsible for all technical development, asset creation, engine integration, and maintenance of the project’s digital infrastructure (website/socials).

Wombi (Stephen): Recognized as a project partner, with credit provided in-game, on the game’s website, and in all official development documentation.

Account Access: Both parties will have shared access to the game’s website and social media channels to ensure transparency.

5. Business Entity & Launch
The game will be launched under an NZ Limited company structure, managed by Boots (Hogan), to optimize tax and legal compliance in New Zealand. All trademarks and brand rights will be held by said entity.

6. Termination & Exit
Should either party choose to terminate this agreement prior to the release of the game:

The project source code and assets remain with Boots (Hogan).

Any remaining portion of the investment (if applicable) and the terms of future rights will be settled in good faith between both parties.
