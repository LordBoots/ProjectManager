# Description:
==================================================
Fighters are the player's primary asset. Each fighter has a unique set of stats, equipment, and training upgrades that can be managed in the Fighters Panel (in Menu Hub -> Fighters).
Fighters make up the players core team that will be used to compete in the arena.
Each fighter needs equipment to compete in the arena. Equipment affects the fighter's reach, damage, speed, and defense capabilities.
Fighters can be trained to improve their stats and abilities.
The User can play as one of the fighters during an arena, if they are downed they can take control of the next fighter in the team.

# Fighter Assignments:
==================================================
**Description**:
    Fighters from your roster can be assigned to two different roles: Arena Team and Envoys.
    These assignments are managed in the Fighters Panel.
    A fighter can be assigned to both roles simultaneously - they serve different purposes at different times.

|--------------|-----|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Assignment   | Max | Purpose                                                              |
|--------------|-----|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Arena Team   | 5   | Scheduled arena fights                                               |
| Envoys       | 3   | Travel protection - fight in combat events                           |
|--------------|-----|----------------------------------------------------------------------|

## Arena Team:
==================================================
The Arena Team is your lineup for scheduled arena fights.
Up to 5 fighters can be assigned to the team at any time.
You control 1 fighter at a time during an arena fight while the others are controlled by the AI.
The Team is displayed in the Personnel Panel under the Arena Team tab.

Assignment:
    - Team slots are displayed alongside the fighter viewport
    - Clicking the [+] button on an empty team slot will add the fighter to the team
    - Clicking the [-] button on a team slot will remove the fighter from the team
    - Clicking the fighter in a team slot will open the fighter management panel for that fighter

## Envoys:
==================================================
Envoys are fighters assigned to protect the player character during travel around the world.
The player character is a non-combatant Region Manager - envoys fight on their behalf when combat events occur in Map Mode.
The envoys are displayed in the Personnel Panel under the Envoys tab.
Up to 3 fighters can be assigned as envoys at any time
The User will be able to control 1 of the envoys at a time during a combat event in Map Mode.
If the player has no envoys assigned they will have to fight by themselves. If they are knocked out they will wake up in the hospital and lose some gold and time will have passed.

Assignment:
    - Assign envoys in the Fighters Panel
    - A Fighters Envoy status is shown in the Fighter

**Risks:**
    - Envoys can be injured during travel combat events in Map Mode.
    - Envoy equipment degrades from travel combat events in Map Mode.
    - Consider the risk: Do you guard with your best fighters (safer travel, but injury risk before scheduled fights)?

**Strategic Considerations:**
    |-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Scenario                      | Implication                                                               |
    |-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Best fighters as envoys       | Safer travel, but risk injury/equipment damage before arena fights        |
    | No envoys assigned            | Risky travel - fight by yourself (Time lost, lose resources, lose gold)   |
    | Weak fighters as guards       | May lose combat events, but arena team is protected                       |
    | Team and Envoys overlap       | Convenient, but all eggs in one basket if injury occurs                   |
    |-------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
 
# Stats:
==================================================
Description:
    Fighters have a set of stats that determine their ability to compete in the arena.
    Stats are affected by training, equipment, buffs and passive buffs.
    Stats are displayed on the left hand side of the fighter management panel.

Stat Types:
    |------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Stat Name        | Description                                                                  |
    |------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Strength         | Affects the damage and defense of the fighters.                              |
    | Dexterity        | Affects the attack and movement speed of the fighters.                       |
    | Endurance        | Affects the fighters stamina loss/gain.                                      |
    |------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

# Equipment:
==================================================
    Fighters need equipment to compete in the arena. Equipment affects the players reach, damage, speed, and defense capabilities.
    The player will have to acquire, maintain, and upgrade equipment to be able to compete in the arena.

# Training:
==================================================
**Description**:
    Fighters can be trained to improve their stats and abilities.
    Training is done in the Keep or through expeditions at the Fighter's Guild.
    Training can only influence a single stat at a time.
    Training results are buffs that are applied to the fighter's stats. They are displayed in the Fighter's Info Panel under the stats.
    Fighters can lose training progress if they are knocked out or die. (removes the buff)

## Basic Training:
==================================================
**Description**:
    Training is done in the keep or through expeditions at the fighter's guild.
    It is a way to increase the stats of the fighters or unlock passive buffs.
    Some types of training are free, others cost resources but are faster and give rewards.

**Starting training:**
    The player can start training their fighters by clicking the "Train" button in the fighter management panel.
    This will open a small dialogue where they can choose the type of training they want to do and what stat to focus on.
    The player can also travel to the fighter's guild to train their fighters.
    Fighters can be trained through expeditions at the fighter's guild.


**Types of Training:**
    Some training types are better than others depending on the stat you are focusing on.
    Some training methods cost resources but are faster and give rewards.
    |--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Training Type            | Description                                                                 |
    |--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Basic Training           | Free and does not require any resources, it just takes time.                |
    | Fighter's Guild Training | Costs resources but are faster and has a chance of giving passive buffs.    |
    | Expedition Training      | Costs resources. Are dangerous but are faster and give the best rewards.    |
    |--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|

**Basic Training:**
    Basic training is free and does not require any resources, it just takes time.
    It is conducted in the keeps courtyard.
    It has the lowest reward and the longest time to complete.
    It is the best way to start training your fighters.

**Fighter's Guild Training:**
    Fighter's guild training costs resources but are faster and have a chance of giving passive buffs.
    It is conducted in the fighter's guild.
    It has the medium reward and the medium time to complete.
    
    The "resource cost" for this training is as stated below:
    - Equipment will degrade slightly. 
    - There is a Gold fee to participate.
    - Fighters cannot be killed. (this would become annoying quickly, also it would be unfair to the player)

**Expedition Training:**
    Expedition training costs resources. Are dangerous but are faster and give the best rewards.
    It is conducted in expeditions which are happen outside of the town.
    It has the highest reward and the shortest time to complete.

    The "resource cost" for this expedition is as stated below:
    - Equipment will degrade slightly. 
    - Fighters can be injured.
    - Equiptment can be damaged badly.
    - Fighters cannot be killed. (this would become annoying quickly)
    


# Buffs:
==================================================
Buffs can be acquired in multiple ways.
They are usually temporary although some acquired through training will be permanent.

UI Display:
    Buffs are displayed in the Fighter's Info Panel under the fighter's stats.
    They appear as an icon that gives more information when hovered over.

Acquisition Methods:
    - Training in the Keep or Fighter's Guild.
    - Training in Expeditions.
    - Events.
    - Potions, tinctures and brews. (these are crafted in the Town's Brewery and Apothecary)

Types of Buffs:
    |--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Buff Type                | Description                                                                 |
    |--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
    | Strength Buffs           | These buffs increase the strength of the fighters.                          |
    | Dexterity Buffs          | These buffs increase the dexterity of the fighters.                         |
    | Endurance Buffs          | These buffs increase the endurance of the fighters.                         |
    | Speed Buffs              | These buffs increase the speed of the fighters.                             |
    |--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
