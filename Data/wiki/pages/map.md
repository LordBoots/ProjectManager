# Main Locations
==================================
Main locations are the buildings that are permanent and house many activities per location. 
They will be visited often by the player and will have changing pools of events and NPC spawns based on the players progression and choices. 

Location List:
- Black Goose Tavern
- Three Coins Inn
- Golden Swan Tavern
- Fighter's Guild
- Arena
- Bank
- Armourer
- Blacksmith
- The Pit
- Docks
- SLums


# Mini Locations:
=====================================
Mini locations are areas on the map that are related to events and missions. 
Mini locations pop up on the map when events are rolled or when quests/missions are taken. 
They could include: Houses to break into, Chests and locks to pick, info targets, quest item sellers and so on. 

For instance: The player takes a mission and then views the map. The map shows that a new quest seller location has opened up. They click it to go there and meet a shady salesman in a back alley.
